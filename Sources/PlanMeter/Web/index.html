PlanMeter

Pair with your Mac

On the Mac, open PlanMeter → Remote and create a pairing code. Scan it here or enter the eight digits shown below the Web QR. Both devices need to be on your tailnet.

  • Added this page to your Home Screen? Pair from here: iOS opens camera-scanned codes in Safari, which keeps its own storage.
  • Pairing codes are single use and expire in 5 minutes.
  • After 5 incorrect attempts, the Mac cancels the code.
  • This browser keeps a non-extractable key; the Mac pins it.
  • Every request is end-to-end encrypted on top of Tailscale and TLS.
